Okay so the meeting confirmed what I was feeling. I need to move on from design and 
begin the data collection stage already. With the guidelines for the week, I started
making the new gripper mount. But during the process I realized that the fingers at
maximum grip also reveal their rack beyond the enclosure. So I need to make the
enclosure wider. In addition, the screw mounts I designed to affix the actuator was 
excessive and two were misaligned as well. So in my third and final edition of the
enclosure, I did the following:
- Added chamfers to the slots to make it easier to slip the actuator into the slots
- Removed the misaligned screw points as they were also excessive
- Added cutouts for the serial wires to passthrough since the actuator will be surrounded
in all dierctions
- Added slots for ease of accessibility to the actuator in case of when replacement
is needed.
- Added M4 screw mounts to affix the GripSense to the existing End-of-arm Tooling
that is already mounted on the VT6 since it has adequate space for accessory mounts.

I 3D printed the enclosure with PLA this time and finally mounted it to the VT6. The
GripSense is ready to collect data! I just need to start the code now. Amazing
progression honestly.
