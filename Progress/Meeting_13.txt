Today's meeting was in person, since Dr. Judhi finally returned to office as the new
semester started. Following up on what he asked me to do last time, I went and found two
papers with genuinely related work, Falco2020Benchmarking and Shang2026FORTE, and did some
proper exploration around them.

That exploration is what made me realise my paper as it stands is actually two papers in
one trenchcoat, and that I have veered away from the path of just building a good gripper
with a good API for anyone to use. The two papers hiding inside mine look something like:
"A self-contained, middleware-free control interface for Dynamixel-actuated parallel
grippers that exposes normalised position and grip-strength commands together with
proprioceptive grasp-state feedback," and "How much slip-detection performance is available
from actuator proprioception alone, with zero added sensing, and how does it vary across
printable finger materials and passive padding?"

When I put this to Dr. Judhi, he agreed I am pulling in two separate directions and that I
need to actually decide whether I pivot toward the benchmarking question or stick with the
control-interface development. I wrapped the meeting up by telling him I want to continue
with the control-interface development and set the material/padding benchmarking aside for
now. So going forward I am treating PLA fingers with rubber-band padding as my working
choice, with an explicit constraint attached: this is an anecdotal choice, not a derived
one, and a proper independent study of finger material and padding choice belongs to future
work. He was fine with that and asked me to reframe my proposal around this decision and
send him the details as soon as I can.
