In my 12th meeting with Dr. Judhi, I was confronted with the two directions that I am taking
my paper. It is clear that I veered off my main path of creating a parallel gripper and into
making a detailed analysis on the best combination of finger and padding. I will only focus
on the gripper and the API moving forward. But I have already started the benchmark testing
and I have only a few data-points to collect. So I might as well collect it and then move 
forward to optimizing the gripper API, testing after optimizations, and finally wrapping up
my report. The benchmark tests pushed me back several weeks and now I have to rush to complete
the rest!

With the API abstracted and the driver split into proper modules, I went back to actually
running the campaign. The experiment design was still hardcoded across a few scripts, so I
lifted it into a single `benchmark_params.yaml`. The materials, paddings, current levels,
and repeat count all live there now, which means I can redefine the matrix without touching
a line of code. Since the fingers had been through calibration, refits, and a module reshuffle
since I last zeroed them, I ran a fresh recalibration on the whole set before trusting any new
numbers against the old ones.

Data collection picked back up from there: rubber-band padding for PLA, PETG, and TPU, and
more of the grip test for ABS.

And ABS is where the week actually went sideways. The ABS fingers are mad annoying. They have
collapsed on me three separate times now, and every time I have just superglued them back
together and kept going rather than reprinting from scratch. This time it finally gave out
mid data collection, right in the middle of the eraser padding cell, which meant throwing away
whatever partial reading I had for that run. At this point I am genuinely debating whether to
just abandon the ABS exploration rather than keep fighting a finger set that cannot survive
its own test matrix. It is one thing for a material to perform badly and another for it to
physically fall apart under me, and I am not sure the second one is telling me anything useful
about grip quality.
