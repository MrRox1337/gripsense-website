My meeting with Dr. Judhi was very useful. I spoke to him about the gripper design and its
limitations. I mentioned that the maximum grip width is about 30mm and it isn't enough to
grip items like the Raspberry Pi SBC or Arduino Uno by its sides. This is when he pointed
out a critical oversight. 

I assumed that the tool center point (TCP) of the end effector is consistent but in reality
it is variable based on the width of the item I manipulate. To hold an object 1mm wide, the
TCP is at the maximum extension but the TCP is a ~10mm behind this point when the object to 
manipulate is 30mm wide. This would mean that a consistent TCP value will not work and I 
need to develop a forumla that will internally adjust the TCP based on the object width.
This added a layer of complexity that I did not expect on the software level but I can deal
with it.
