Today I discussed with Dr. Judhi, the topic of my project. I want to work on
my existing project, the Picker-Bot, and deploy it in the real world as the current
version only exists in simulation. One of the limiting factors of real world deployment
is the elimination of the assumption that "the object will be in hand and out of it when
the certain state is expected." To eliminate this, I decided to explore literature to 
figure out what kinds of end-effectors exists and what was commonly used in literature
to build end-effectors for pick-place tangible objects, ideally PCBs as my scope for 
this end-effector is manipulating microelectronic circuit modules like microcontrollers,
sensors, and actuators.
